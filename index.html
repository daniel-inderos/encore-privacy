Encore Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026. Encore is an independent Spotify companion for a private beta. This policy describes the app, not the separate Spotify service.

Spotify account and music

With your permission, Encore connects directly to Spotify to display your profile, library, playlists, listening history and current playback, and to carry out playback and library actions you request. Spotify receives these requests and processes them under its own privacy policy. Encore does not ask for or store your Spotify password.

Secure sign-in and local storage

Spotify access and refresh tokens are stored in the device Keychain. Preferences are saved on your device. Music metadata and lyrics are cached in memory while the app runs. Artwork may also be cached on disk to reduce downloads. Encore does not operate a developer server for your account data, sell your data, or include advertising or cross-app tracking.

Lyrics and other providers

To find lyrics, Encore sends song titles, artists, album names and durations to Better Lyrics and LRCLIB, and Spotify track identifiers to GitHub to retrieve the AMLL TTML Database. These providers receive your network address and ordinary request information. Encore does not send them your Spotify tokens or password. Providers control their own server logs and retention; their policies and terms apply to data they receive. Spotify artwork and its playback SDK are fetched from Spotify services.

Disconnecting and deletion

Sign Out in Settings removes Encore’s stored Spotify tokens and clears its account state and content caches. It does not delete your Spotify account, change your playlists or erase data Spotify or another provider already holds. To revoke Encore’s permission at Spotify, open your Spotify account’s Apps page and choose Remove access for the connected app. Removing Encore deletes its app container; signing out first also removes its Keychain credentials. Local display preferences do not contain your music history.

Beta diagnostics and contact

If you choose to send TestFlight feedback, it is handled through Apple and may include screenshots or diagnostics you provide. Avoid including private information in feedback. Contact Daniel Hasbun at daniel.inderos@icloud.com for support or privacy requests about Encore. Direct requests about data held by Spotify or a lyrics provider to that provider.
